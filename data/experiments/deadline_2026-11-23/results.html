ScamBench: completed action prediction and diffusion experiments

23 November 2026. Full local CPU runs: seed 42; 30 MLP epochs and 30 diffusion epochs; 100 diffusion steps; 8 samples per prediction. Both heads use identical train-fitted TF-IDF/SVD features.

Mixed training: 11,227 English plus 3,600 silver Kazakh rows. Validation: 1,208 English plus 399 silver rows. Evaluation: 300 paired conversations per language, belonging to 273 families. Families do not cross these partitions.

Intervals use 2,000 paired resamples of conversation families. This preserves dependencies between restylings. Intervals containing zero do not establish an improvement.

Limits: one seed; a historical benchmark that informed earlier decisions; no native-speaker review; only five action types evaluated, including just one ignore example. Action prediction measures agreement with recorded actions, not execution safety. The diffusion implementation adapts CARD and is not a reproduction. XLM-R is not reported here: the remote Windows machine was still installing CUDA PyTorch at the last update. These new baselines reserve development families and differ from the earlier 0.687/0.898 experiments.
